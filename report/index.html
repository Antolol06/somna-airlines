SOMNAPhase 1 · Brainstorming
Phase 1

Brainstorming
Product Ideas

To begin with, we uploaded the original Final Project.pdf file (containing the brief/assignment for the final project) into various Frontier AI Models (Claude, Chat GPT, Gemini). What became immediately apparent is that, despite the fact that the document clearly specifies that the idea should be original and creative, there was significant and noticeable convergence in the outputs that the different AI models provided.

Specifically, some concepts, such as Reliving Memories and Dreams came up again and again. Even specific products (such as Nostalgia perfume or Dreamflix) were presented multiple times by different models.

1. NOSTALGIA – Memories in a Bottle

A perfume that allows you to physically relive a memory through scent. Customers could bottle their childhood, a summer holiday or their first love.

Slogan: Some moments deserve to last forever.

Target: Sentimental consumers and luxury shoppers.

2. Dreamflix – Netflix for Your Dreams

A subscription service that lets you select what you dream about before falling asleep. Choose between adventure, romance, fantasy or custom experiences.

Slogan: Your night. Your story.

Target: Young adults and entertainment enthusiasts.

SOMNAPhase 1 · Brainstorming

Some models, notably Chat GPT, also provided the user with a series of graphs using completely arbitrary or subject values in order to gauge the suitability of certain ideas for our particular project. These values were presented with little-to-no explanation of what they actually mean, no specific scaling, etc.

Only once we increased the specificity of our prompts through prompt engineering techniques (i.e. specifying that the product/service should solve a real-world, tangible problem in a unique way, etc.), we were able to generate some genuinely unique and useful output.

We ended up settling on the idea of an airline that would allow you to sleep in pods during your flight (inspired by the idea of those famous Japanese pod hotels). We would continue to incorporate imagery of these pods into our prompts in order to give the AI a tangible idea of what it had to generate.

SOMNAPhase 2 · The website
Phase 2

Generating
the Website

We first asked Chat GPT to write a prompt-engineered, thoroughly detailed prompt that matches our idea and generates a corresponding corporate identity around it. We chose to let the prompt be inspired by scrolling Apple website presentations, as well as the oryzo.ai website’s scrolling design. Chat GPT specified a preferred technology stack in order to realistically render the website’s opening sequence.

Prompt · to Chat GPT

You need to build to me a prompt to create an interactive 3d website style (like apple presentation ones or like this one https://oryzo.ai/). The idea is to create a website for an airline that makes you sleep when you get inside. it is a premium ariline with capsule like japanese pods. the website should like make you select where to go and i don't know do something beatufull that makes you say WOW

When subsequently pasting the prompt into Claude, the result was a promising start to our website with some notable functional and visual errors. For example, the 3d world that appears when selecting a destination was flickering, detracting from the premium feel of the website.

What must also be mentioned is that rendering the whole experience, expectedly, took significant time, over 1 hour. Also, implementing the refinements would take another half-a-day because of usage limitations from Claude.

When we were finally able to make all necessary refinements, we again chose to allow Chat GPT to generate a detailed prompt for Claude. This, we feel, made the error correction much more efficient, as Chat GPT was able to provide precise instructions on the refinements that needed to be made.

SOMNAPhase 3 · The presentation
Phase 3

Generating
the Presentation

After we were fully satisfied with how the website behaved when the user interacted with it, we asked Claude to build a presentation based upon the details of the website, again based upon an initial prompt made by Chat GPT. The final prompt included elements such as:

Prompt · excerpt
  • Visual identity and branding;
  • Typography and colour palette;
  • Animations and transitions;
  • 3D environments and visual assets;
  • Tone of voice;
  • Overall design philosophy.

The presentation must feel like a natural extension of the website, belonging to the same brand and visual ecosystem.

2. Presentation Format

Build the presentation as a standalone, interactive, web-based slide deck rather than a traditional PowerPoint.

Take full advantage of modern web technologies to create an experience that would be difficult or impossible to achieve with conventional presentation software.

Claude was able to near seamlessly integrate the core components of the offering (colour palette, corporate identity and design philosophy) into an initial build of the presentation. However, the 3D animation which were taken from the website were very laggy and thus visually unappealing.

For this reason, we asked Claude to refine the animations, making the animations much more visually appealing.

Subsequently, both the presentation and the website were deployed via Netlify.

You can find the presentation here: https://somnaairlines.netlify.app/presentation/

SOMNAFinal evaluation

Final Evaluation
of Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Fast output (subject to how demanding the task is)
  • Increased productivity (possibility to subsequently work on multiple tasks)
  • Easy interface due to simple chat mechanics
  • Capable of asking clarifying question intermittently

Cons

  • Large output convergence, giving rise to AI slop
  • False sense of confidence in presenting AI slop as unique and original
  • Presentation of arbitrary/subjective values with little-to-no added value
  • There are time and token limit-related constraints
  • Requirement to point out flaws in the output.
